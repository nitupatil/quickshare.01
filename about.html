About Us 🚀

Quickshares.online was built with a single goal: to make transferring files between devices as frictionless as possible. No accounts, no subscriptions, and no complicated setups. Just select your files, get a code, and download them instantly.

The Developer

Built by Nitish Badak, a web developer based in Maharashtra, India. With a focus on creating custom web applications and business solutions, Quickshares is a showcase of utilizing modern client-side architecture and cloud databases to solve everyday workflow problems.

How It Works

Your files are securely uploaded directly to our cloud bucket. Once downloaded, or after exactly 5 minutes, they are permanently destroyed. We believe in privacy by design.
