How to share files with a QR code

A QR code can save the receiver from typing a long link. With Quickshares, create a temporary transfer on one device and scan its QR code using another device.

Start a free file transfer →See complete instructions

No account • Up to 50 MB per transfer • Sessions last up to 5 minutes

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Select Send Files

    On the sending laptop or phone, open Quickshares and choose Send Files.

  2. Upload your files

    Choose files up to 50 MB combined, set a four-digit PIN and select a download limit.

  3. Display the QR code

    After a successful upload, Quickshares shows a temporary QR code along with your PIN and OTP.

  4. Scan and download

    On the other device, open its camera or QR scanner, scan the code, follow the link to Quickshares, and download while the transfer remains active.

When QR sharing is most convenient

QR sharing works especially well when the sending device has a screen and the receiving device has a camera. For example, upload a document on your laptop and scan the QR code from your phone. You can also display the code on one phone and scan it with another.

Phone to laptop? Most laptop setups do not have a convenient built-in QR scanner. Instead, select Receive Files on the laptop and enter the displayed PIN and OTP. Read our phone-to-laptop transfer guide.

What if the QR code does not scan?

Turn up the sending screen's brightness, move your camera farther from or closer to the code, and ensure the whole code is in frame. If scanning still fails, open Quickshares on the receiver, choose Receive Files, and enter the PIN and OTP manually.

The QR code represents a temporary transfer, so you still need to download before the session expires. Quickshares sessions last up to five minutes and can end sooner when the allowed number of downloads has been reached.

Keep QR codes private

Do not publish a transfer QR code to a public group or social feed. It can contain the temporary credentials needed to access the file. Show it only to your intended recipient, and avoid sharing sensitive documents on shared or untrusted devices.

Can I share more than one file using the same QR code?

Yes. Select multiple files for the transfer, provided their total size does not exceed 50 MB.

Does a QR code stay active forever?

No. The sharing session lasts up to five minutes and can end earlier when its download limit is reached.

Can the receiver skip typing a PIN?

Scanning the generated QR code opens the temporary receiver link with the transfer details. If the scan fails, the receiver can enter the PIN and OTP manually.

Do I need to register or download an app?

No account or additional Quickshares app is needed. Use the web browser and a camera or QR scanner on the receiving device.
