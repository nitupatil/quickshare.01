How it works

Transfer files between devices in 3 easy steps

Quickshares helps you move files from one device to another without making an account or installing another app. All you need is a modern browser and an internet connection.

📁

1. Choose your files

Select Send Files and pick documents, photos or other files from your device. You can add multiple files as long as the combined size is 50 MB or less.

🔑

2. Set a temporary PIN

Enter a four-digit PIN and choose a download limit. After the upload finishes, Quickshares shows a QR code and an OTP to use on the receiving device.

📲

3. Receive your files

Scan the QR code or open Quickshares on another device, tap Receive Files, and enter the PIN and OTP. The sharing session lasts up to five minutes.

Common questions

File sharing, made straightforward

Can I share files online without signing up?

Yes. Quickshares does not require you to create an account to send or receive files. Use the Send Files and Receive Files options on the homepage.

How can I transfer files from my phone to my laptop?

Open Quickshares on your phone, tap Send Files, choose files and create a transfer. On your laptop, open the site and enter the displayed PIN and OTP. You can also use the QR code when your receiving device can scan it.

What is the maximum file size?

Quickshares currently supports up to 50 MB in total per transfer, including when you select multiple files.

How long does a file transfer stay available?

The sharing session is designed to expire after five minutes. It may end sooner if it reaches the maximum number of downloads you selected.

Do I need to install an app to receive files?

No. Open Quickshares in a modern web browser, select Receive Files, and enter the PIN and OTP shown to the sender.

Ready to share a file?

No account, no extra download. Start a temporary transfer in your browser.
