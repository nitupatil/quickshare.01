How it Works ✦

Quickshares makes transferring files between your phone, laptop, and friends completely frictionless.

📤 1. Select & Secure

Drop your files into the upload zone (up to 50MB). Set a temporary 4-digit PIN to secure your transfer and choose how many times the file can be downloaded.

🚀 2. Get Your Code

Click send! Your files are instantly encrypted and temporarily stored in the cloud. We will give you a magic QR code and a 4-digit OTP.

📥 3. Catch Your Files

On your receiving device, simply scan the QR code with your camera to instantly open the file vault. Or, go to Quickshares.site and manually enter your PIN and OTP.

💨 4. Auto-Destruct

Once your timer expires (5 minutes) or your maximum download limit is reached, your files are permanently deleted from our servers forever.
