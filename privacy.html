Privacy Policy 🔒

At Quickshares.online, your privacy and data security are our top priorities. Because we are a temporary file-sharing service, our philosophy is simple: we keep as little data as possible, for the shortest time possible.

1. Temporary File Storage

Any file you upload is stored temporarily in our secure cloud storage. Files are automatically and permanently deleted from our servers when:

  • The 5-minute session timer expires.
  • The maximum download limit you set is reached.
  • The sender manually clicks "End Session".

2. Data Access & Security

We do not index, search, view, or analyze the contents of the files you upload. Files can only be accessed by someone who possesses both the correct 4-digit PIN (set by you) and the dynamically generated 4-digit OTP.

3. Information Collection

We do not require user accounts, emails, or passwords to use Quickshares. We do not sell any data to third parties. We only collect basic analytical data (like file sizes and upload counts) strictly to monitor server performance and limits.

4. User Responsibility

Please remember that while our links are secure and self-destructing, you are responsible for who you share your PIN and OTP with. Do not share illegal, copyrighted, or malicious files through our service.
