Privacy Policy 🔒

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

1. The 5-Minute Data Destruction Policy

Quickshares.online is designed for temporary file sharing. Any file you upload is kept in our secure storage bucket for a maximum of 5 minutes, or until your specified download limit is reached. Once expired, the files are permanently and automatically deleted from our servers. We do not keep backups of your uploaded files.

2. Information We Collect

  • Uploaded Files: Temporarily stored strictly for the purpose of facilitating the transfer.
  • System Logs: We may temporarily log IP addresses and browser types to prevent abuse, spam, or malicious uploads.
  • Usage Data: We track anonymous download counts to enforce the download limits you set.

3. No Accounts Required

We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers to use the core service. You do not need to register to send or receive files.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We use session storage locally on your browser to remember if you have seen our intro animation and to ensure a smooth user experience. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising.
